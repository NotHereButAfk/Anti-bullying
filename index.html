    AFTER THE BELL · CHAPTER EDITION

    Your stories

    Saved in this browser. Export a file to keep a separate copy or move to another device.

    Your previous edition’s save is left untouched. Find the earlier edition in the repository to continue it.

    Make yourself comfortable

    There are no time limits or penalties for pausing. Choices can also be selected with number keys when you are not typing.

    Operating-system reduced-motion preferences are respected automatically.

    You can take your time.

    The story waits here. There is no penalty for taking a break, rewinding a choice, or starting with a different perspective.

    If these experiences feel familiar, you deserve support from someone you trust. You do not have to handle bullying alone.

    A story with room to change

    Characters remember specific choices. Some paths converge, but conversations, consequences, and epilogues can differ. Millions of fully unique written stories are not claimed.

    This is authored fiction, not a clinical tool or a model of what every school will do. Bullying is never the target’s fault. Forgiveness is not owed, and a quiet boundary is not a failed ending.

    Everything runs locally. Nothing you type is sent to a server by the game. Avoid using real classmates’ private information.
